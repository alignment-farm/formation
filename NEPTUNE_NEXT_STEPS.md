# Neptune-Style Theory: Findings and Next Steps

Status: **conceptual research review; not a Formation finding, lifecycle
advance, code license, or model-contact license**.

This review covers the root [README](README.md) and the current Markdown
authority set under [`docs/`](docs/README.md). The review may sharpen the
research program, but it does not move the present `environment_application`
boundary or satisfy any predecessor required by the deterministic fixture.

## Verdict

Formation is tracking unusually well to the standard needed for serious
theory-first research. It already does several things that most memory-system
projects do not:

- it names an object of study rather than starting from a product category;
- it treats every arrow from experience to later influence as questionable;
- it distinguishes occurrence, interpretation, governance, influence, action,
  consequence, and experimental verdict;
- it makes the harness/runtime boundary a condition of identifiability;
- it permits ordinary persistence and static instruction to win;
- it requires transfer, non-transfer, counterevidence, costs, and causal
  ablation before a bounded Formation claim;
- it explicitly classifies deterministic fixture success as wire conformance,
  not learning evidence; and
- it has recognized its own model-admission selection effect and restored the
  deterministic lifecycle route.

That is a strong research constitution. It is not yet a Neptune-style theory.
Most of the current corpus specifies what a valid observation would have to
look like and builds apparatus capable of preserving it. It rarely derives a
claim from stated premises, proves that rival explanations are
indistinguishable under some observation boundary, or predicts a result not
used to construct the apparatus.

The gap is therefore not “more implementation” or “more rigor.” It is a
missing analytical layer between the concept and the experiment.

## What Neptune-style means here

Neptune-style theory is Markdown-first analytical research in which an
unobserved consequence is derived from a small set of explicit premises and
then exposed to a future observation that could make the derivation lose.

The analogy should be kept exact. Neptune was not inferred from a rich
vocabulary for planets. It was inferred because an existing theory, prior
observations, and a discrepancy jointly constrained where another cause would
have to be. Formation therefore earns a Neptune-style result only when it has:

1. observations or already-earned findings that precede the derivation;
2. explicit assumptions and definitions;
3. a derivation that an independent reader can reconstruct;
4. countermodels showing why weaker premises do not suffice;
5. a prospective prediction not selected after seeing the result; and
6. a later observation capable of confirming, narrowing, or killing the claim.

Markdown can be the complete research instrument for steps 1 through 5. Code
is needed only when the predicted observation requires computation or contact.
The absence of code does not make an analytical result preliminary. Conversely,
formal-looking prose is not theory merely because it is precise.

## The missing epistemic layer

Formation currently distinguishes semantic review, code licensing, wire
conformance, and scientific evidence. Add one distinct status family:

```text
definition
assumption
analytical proposition
derived prediction
empirical finding
```

These statuses must not promote into one another automatically.

- A definition fixes usage; it cannot be confirmed by a fixture.
- An assumption is a declared premise; implementation does not prove it.
- An analytical proposition follows from premises; it may be valid before
  model contact.
- A derived prediction exposes the proposition to a future observation.
- An empirical finding reports what happened under a frozen contact boundary.

This would let Formation recognize genuine blackboard results without calling
them empirical findings, and recognize immaculate instrumentation without
calling it theory.

## First theoretical object

The first theory packet should answer one narrow question:

> What observations can distinguish a formed practitioner from ordinary
> explicit persistence, static instruction, and harness-assisted prompting?

This is upstream of mechanism choice. It also directly addresses the project's
central risk: inheriting the word *formation* from its own vocabulary rather
than earning a distinct causal object.

Create `docs/NEPTUNE_THEORY.md` only when this packet is ready. It should be a
short derivation, not another lifecycle contract. Give it the following fixed
sections:

1. **Prior observations** — only empirical findings or retained anomalies that
   genuinely precede the theory, with Construct provenance where applicable.
2. **Definitions** — the minimum observational terms needed by the argument.
3. **Assumptions** — especially the action observation boundary, coldness, and
   what counts as the same foreground.
4. **Propositions** — numbered claims with short derivations.
5. **Countermodels** — systems that pass weaker tests without forming.
6. **Predictions** — prospective directional expectations.
7. **Disconfirmation** — results that would narrow or kill each proposition.
8. **Instrument consequences** — only the apparatus forced by those
   predictions.

Do not add schemas, receipt types, authorities, or lifecycle transitions unless
one of the derivations proves that the distinction is necessary for the stated
observation.

## Candidate propositions to attempt, not assume

These are promising theorem candidates. They are not findings and should not
enter the concept vocabulary as facts until their assumptions and proofs have
survived independent attack.

### P1 — Action-boundary equivalence

If two practitioners have the same cold model, tools, inference settings, and
complete action-relevant request distribution, and their only causal path to
action is through that request, then a behavioral evaluator cannot identify
which hidden persistence history produced the action.

Consequence: a claimed hidden developmental state is not behaviorally distinct
merely because its lineage differs. Formation must predict a difference in
selection, scope, cost, revision, or later action under a prospectively chosen
contrast.

### P2 — Oracle-substitution non-identifiability

If the harness may use hidden case construction or future labels to author the
candidate, its applicability, or its admission, then the observed treatment
effect is compatible with oracle-assisted prompting and does not identify
practitioner formation.

The authority documents already enforce the practical version of this claim.
The theory packet should derive exactly which information flows make the two
causal accounts observationally equivalent.

### P3 — Positive-transfer insufficiency

A positive acquisition/transfer pair alone cannot distinguish selective
formation from a broad instruction that happens to cover both cases.
Non-transfer and current-state-sufficient cases are therefore identifying
contrasts, not merely quality checks.

### P4 — Revision insufficiency of one-shot success

One improved later action cannot establish a durable, revisable practitioner
change. The same observation is compatible with transient context use,
unscoped rule following, and cached provider continuity. Counterevidence and a
later post-revision decision are needed to identify the stronger object.

### P5 — Governance-value localization

When raw persistence and a governed candidate expose equivalent useful content
on positive cases, any value unique to governance should appear primarily in
silence, adverse-transfer avoidance, correction, revocation, or cost—not in
the positive case itself.

This last proposition is especially suitable for a risky prediction. A future
result in which governance wins only on positive cases while tying or losing on
all selectivity and revision cases should count against the claimed purpose of
governance.

## Required countermodels

The theory packet should construct at least these countermodels in prose before
new contact:

- **Authored-wire system:** the present deterministic fixture passes every
  lifecycle boundary although no model learns. This already proves that valid
  lineage and Formation are different objects.
- **Perfect episodic retriever:** raw recall supplies all action-relevant
  information a governed candidate would supply, at equal or lower cost.
- **Static expert instruction:** the practitioner acts correctly without any
  acquisition experience.
- **Overbroad lesson:** a rule improves the positive case and harms a
  superficially similar non-transfer case.
- **Label-leaking selector:** a harness uses family identity to look perfectly
  selective while the practitioner has acquired nothing.
- **Provider-continuity system:** undeclared session state produces apparent
  durability without retained developmental lineage.

For each one, state which current Formation observation it can counterfeit and
which prospective contrast separates it.

## Prospective predictions worth freezing

The first predictions should be structural and directional rather than claims
that a chosen model will make a convenient error.

1. Strong raw persistence should match a governed candidate on at least some
   positive cases; otherwise the raw comparator is probably weak or the offers
   are not information-matched.
2. The first plausible advantage of governance should be fewer unwarranted
   activations or faster recovery from counterevidence, not universal positive
   accuracy.
3. Ambiguous consequences should produce unstable or appropriately withheld
   candidate admission. A mechanism that confidently derives one lesson from
   every ambiguous outcome should incur negative transfer.
4. Exact provenance and identity machinery should change the validity of
   causal attribution, but should not by itself predict better task outcomes.
5. A candidate policy chosen using contacted benchmark errors should transfer
   less reliably than a policy whose scope follows from a pre-contact causal
   account, once lexical overlap is removed.

Each prediction needs a named losing observation. Do not freeze all five as one
experiment; select the smallest one that constrains the next contact.

## Keep two research lanes separate

### Lane A — analytical theory

This lane may proceed in Markdown while the deterministic lifecycle is built.
Its outputs are definitions, assumptions, propositions, countermodels, and
predictions. It advances Formation only when it changes what observation is
needed or proves that an apparatus distinction is necessary. Page count,
independent prose agreement, and additional vocabulary are not progress by
themselves.

### Lane B — observational apparatus

Continue the reviewed route already named in [`docs/PLAN.md`](docs/PLAN.md):

1. implement the licensed environment-application composition;
2. implement consequence intake and positive experience closure;
3. traverse deterministic non-transfer, counterevidence, suspension,
   revocation, and post-revocation silence;
4. complete source-sensitive constrained replay and the ablation path; and
5. return to model contact only when a lifecycle boundary names an inference
   responsibility the deterministic actor cannot perform.

Lane A must not call itself completion of any Lane B boundary. Lane B must not
call conformance evidence support for a Lane A proposition.

## Operational finding from the current corpus

The completed model-contact branch is careful and honestly bounded. Its results
are valid for their exact interfaces: two Composer tasks were not engaged;
several small-model setups were contract- or computation-unreliable; structured
output repaired shape without repairing answers. None is Formation evidence.

The scale of that branch nevertheless matters. A large fraction of the corpus
and runner effort was spent selecting a practitioner that would be weak in the
right way while remaining experimentally convenient. The plan correctly names
the resulting selection effect: rigorous non-contact displaced developmental
uncertainty.

Do not erase those packets; they are valuable negative research history. Do
change how they are presented:

- keep `docs/README.md` divided conceptually into current authority,
  deterministic apparatus, closed contact history, and normative appendices;
- treat completed admission/interface packets as an archive of route failure,
  not an active ladder awaiting another model;
- report their aggregate methodological lesson once rather than allowing every
  exact packet to dominate the current research narrative; and
- do not begin another admission or model-search packet under a different
  checkpoint name.

## Difference from community memory work

Most leaderboard and academic memory projects begin with an implemented memory
operation—add, retrieve, summarize, consolidate, rewrite, or optimize—and ask
whether the resulting system performs better. Formation begins with an
identifiability problem: what would justify saying that consequence changed a
practitioner rather than merely changing its next prompt?

That difference is real, but vocabulary does not secure it. A candidate,
admission, activation, or revocation pipeline can still be an ordinary
context-management system. Formation becomes distinct only if its theory and
prospective contrasts rule out the simpler accounts.

Community systems should therefore supply:

- strong explicit-persistence baselines;
- concrete countermodels for allegedly developmental behavior;
- candidate mechanisms and failure modes; and
- external pressure against an artificially weak retrieval control.

They should not supply Formation's ontology. Conversely, Formation's elaborate
authority and provenance apparatus should not be presumed superior to a
community system until a bounded result shows that the distinction changes a
scientific verdict or outcome.

## Immediate sequence

1. Keep `environment_application` as the active lifecycle route and complete
   its already licensed narrow implementation.
2. In parallel only at the conceptual level, draft P1 and P2 with explicit
   causal diagrams, assumptions, proofs, and countermodels.
3. Ask an independent critic to defeat the derivations, not merely reconstruct
   their vocabulary.
4. Promote at most one surviving proposition into one prospective prediction.
5. Let that prediction select the next experimental contrast and only the
   apparatus it actually requires.
6. Revisit the Formation concept vocabulary after the first theory packet.
   Retain terms that do identifying work; demote terms that only name the
   authored fixture path.

## Stop conditions

Stop or redirect the work when:

- a definition is presented as a discovery;
- independent readers agree on prose but have not tried to produce a
  countermodel;
- a fixture-authored distinction is promoted into a universal lifecycle;
- a theorem depends on the conclusion hidden inside the definition of
  *Formation*;
- a prediction is written after the relevant output is known;
- more protocol detail is added without changing an identifying contrast;
- another model search replaces contact with developmental uncertainty;
- exact provenance is credited with behavioral benefit rather than validity;
- a theory document starts specifying services, schemas, or product features;
  or
- a successful community mechanism is renamed with Formation vocabulary and
  treated as support for the thesis.

The desired result is not a grand architecture proven by Markdown. It is a
small body of analytical constraints and risky predictions that tell the lab
where an unobserved Formation effect would have to appear—and what observation
would show that it is not there.
