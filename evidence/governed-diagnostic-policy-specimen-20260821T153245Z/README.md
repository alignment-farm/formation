# Governed diagnostic policy specimen

This zero-call specimen asks whether retained signal records can govern access
to a costly diagnostic without letting the harness choose a task action.

The mechanism conforms. The runtime governor authorized the diagnostic when
the public two-signal alphabet had exactly one admitted record per signal. It
withheld when the retained records were removed and when both public signals
were unfamiliar. Withholding invoked no model, applied no environment action,
and left the service window available.

Correctness did not enter the authorization. Learned, supplied, and reversed
records all received `authorize_diagnostic`. Both hidden profiles then consumed
the service window and emitted a signal. Exact matching selected the first or
second retained record. The deterministic interpreter completed both tasks
with learned and supplied records and failed both with reversed records. That
later split is the evidence that the governor checked coverage, not whether a
record would produce the right task action.

The learned inputs are not fixture labels. They are reconstructed from the two
admitted records in the retained asymmetric-probe clerk packet with SHA-256
`4c7bf10b7ea53abe4f1a9b4ddd477674b07101fc92139c37ed9abc2c2a06f005`.
The policy received the public device state and those admitted records only.
Hidden profile, emitted signal, valid control, branch, and score were absent
from every authorization input and receipt.

All nine malformed, ambiguous, stale, and mismatched cases failed closed.
Reversing record order did not change a decision, inputs remained unchanged,
and replay reproduced the exact packet. The packet SHA-256 is
`a8e6df51e6eb644d4ecf6403e153a1476124b0220abb053982a8ead615ac3fcd`.

This is a mechanism result, not a behavioral result. It shows that acquired
records can govern whether the system opens a costly information encounter.
It does not show that the cold model chose to seek information, learned its
value, or improved. A live first-action comparison would merely reproduce the
frozen policy decision. Further participant contact is worthwhile only if it
tests a new uncertainty after the signal, not to validate this deterministic
authorization again.
