# Post-accounting authorship boundary

Status: **independently review-stable analytical boundary after the completed
occurrence-accounting contact; a separate route-selection artifact is licensed,
but no mechanism, instrument repair, cases, budget, implementation, runner, or
model call is licensed**.

## Main point

The occurrence-accounting contact did not isolate an account-delivery effect.
It also did not reduce the result to one failure.

Three different observations must remain separate:

1. **Pre-account authorship.** Direct, withheld, and static-expanded requests
   returned the same stable candidate in each world. Those candidates were
   shaped as acquisition control tokens rather than reusable changes.
2. **Account and restatement behavior.** Both faithful accounts repeated the
   acquisition controls. Delivered and deterministic-restatement candidates
   sometimes contained longer relational prose, but neither condition was
   stable. The packet cannot attribute token attraction, abstraction, or
   variance to account persistence.
3. **Instrument limits.** Exact-byte content controls missed delivered prompt
   length by 58 and 52 provider tokens, and three combined same-response calls
   exhausted their completion ceiling before valid JSON. Those failures made
   comparisons unavailable; they are not developmental results.

The next question is therefore route selection, not production of one preferred
candidate shape:

> Which single causal pressure, if any, should Formation study next—generic
> responsibility legibility, repeated-source salience, model-authored
> challenge or revision, or model-chosen additional experience—and what weak or
> null observation would distinguish it from another sample, static guidance,
> draft replay, occurrence repetition, and model-free restatement?

Exact acquisition-control copying remains an observable eligibility failure,
not the definition of authorship success. A non-copying candidate may still be
wrong, overbroad, static-instruction-equivalent, or useless. Family and phase
identifiers may be necessary to state warranted scope. The problem document
does not provide a model answer or a preferred vocabulary for a reusable
candidate.

This remains an authorship-only problem. Candidate admission, later action,
transfer, and Formation are downstream questions.

## Evidence that created this boundary

The completed
[occurrence-accounting record](../evidence/occurrence-accounting-exploratory-contact-20260818/README.md)
contains 37 calls with no retry. Both accounts were nonempty. Twenty-nine
candidate outputs were parseable and all were refused by the frozen governor;
three same-response outputs were truncated before a candidate existed.

Neither world had an available eight-condition comparison:

- direct and withheld were internally stable, byte-identical requests with
  exact output matches;
- delivered was unstable in both worlds;
- same-response was unavailable in both worlds;
- restatement and content control were unstable in both worlds;
- World E repeated occurrence was also unstable;
- World F repeated occurrence was stable and differed from direct only by its
  copied `movement_direction` counterevidence field; and
- prompt-mass comparison was unavailable in both worlds.

No exact collapse label and no weak delivery-conditioned label issued. The
contact supports no claim that occurrence accounting worked, failed in
general, caused token copying, or created an abstraction.

## The research problem and the instrument debt

The research problem is what model-authored developmental responsibility, if
any, can be made legible without the apparatus supplying its answer. The live
record does not distinguish responsibility ambiguity, episode and field-name
salience, one-shot compression, sampling variance, or an inference limit. The
apparatus may preserve and compare model work. It may not perform that work.

The instrument debt is narrower:

- byte length was a poor proxy for tokenizer mass in this packet; and
- one 256-token response was often too small for a full account plus candidate.

Repairing either instrument could make a comparator observable. It would not
make a candidate less token-bound, more correct, more stable, or more useful.
Conversely, leaving an instrument broken cannot establish a null about the
research mechanism. Future work must say which problem it addresses before it
changes anything.

## Facts that a next proposal must not rewrite

- Token-shaped direct authorship preceded account generation and delivery.
- A faithful natural-language account may legitimately mention episode
  identifiers. Their presence is not itself an account error. Both live
  accounts remain semantically `indeterminate`; either may already contain a
  reusable relation despite the lexical copying observation.
- Longer or more relational prose appeared under more than one condition and
  was unstable. It is not an account-delivery result.
- Governor refusal is eligibility evidence, not empirical counterevidence or a
  competence score.
- The exact-byte content control did what it promised at the byte surface. Its
  token disparity is a disclosed limit, not request drift.
- Same-response truncation is an interface-cost observation. Increasing its
  budget after seeing the result would create a successor instrument, not
  repair the consumed record.
- The Qwen artifact participated. Awkward behavior does not reopen admission or
  model search.

## Apparent solutions that answer for the model

Do not count any of these as model-authored developmental work:

- a schema with fields for phase-to-slot mapping, inverse control roles, or the
  realized family profile;
- an instruction that says which acquisition controls should be rewritten as
  which transferable roles;
- a runtime transform that deletes control tokens and substitutes `first`,
  `second`, `increasing`, or `decreasing` in their place;
- a scorer that chooses the most relation-like draft from repeated samples;
- a governor message that returns the missing relation as a correction;
- another guaranteed-identifying occurrence presented as though the first
  occurrence lacked the relation;
- a larger completion budget described as improved authorship; or
- a tokenizer-matched control described as evidence of development.

Shape constraints may keep requests observable. They remain instrumentation.

## Open causal routes

No route is selected. A later selection may consider at least:

1. **Responsibility legibility.** The short direct outputs suggest that the
   generic `change` responsibility may have been read as a request for an
   action token. A different generic responsibility would be a static-prompt
   intervention. It may not name episode/relation separation, slot vocabulary,
   inverse roles, or the desired candidate form, and it needs a call-matched
   static ceiling.
2. **Model-authored challenge or revision.** A later cold call may inspect an
   exact prior account or candidate and challenge it without receiving the
   governor's answer. Draft replay, another call, and another sample remain
   separate pressures.
3. **Model-chosen additional experience.** The practitioner may request an
   environment probe whose consequence could disambiguate or test its own
   account. This changes the experience history and must not be described as a
   better interpretation of the first occurrence.
4. **Analytical stop.** Review may find that every live route currently answers
   for the model or changes too many causes. That closes this selection
   boundary without a contact. If one route is later selected, its own bounded
   null must complete that route rather than trigger a staircase through the
   remaining options.

These routes may not be combined merely to increase the chance of a convenient
candidate.

## Comparator pressure before selection

Any research route must expose, rather than erase, these explanations:

- an independent sample of the exact direct responsibility;
- a call-count-matched static instruction carrying the same generic guidance;
- exact draft or account replay without the proposed new operation;
- exact occurrence repetition without a new environment event;
- deterministic restatement of already-present occurrence facts; and
- the natural cost of visible prompt tokens, completion tokens, calls, and
  malformed outputs.

If a route claims that runtime retention matters, it must still confront a
same-call generated-intermediate explanation. If that comparator cannot be
made observable under a pre-frozen fair budget, the retention-specific claim
remains unavailable. A larger budget must apply symmetrically to every
responsibility whose output burden it is meant to compare.

Prompt-token parity is not available merely because byte lengths match. A
future mass control must be specified without deriving favorable content from
the live account, and failure to achieve its predeclared parity leaves prompt
mass live. The project does not need to repair that control before exploring a
weaker question, but it may not use strong prompt-content language without it.

Candidate equality and candidate quality remain separate. Exact wording can
expose stability and collapse. A scorer may classify content after contact but
may not select, rewrite, deliver, or govern a candidate.

Stability is a condition for exact comparison language, not a successful
authorship event. Lower temperature, more samples, more completion tokens, or a
shorter schema changes instrumentation or sampling. Such changes must be
frozen symmetrically and cannot turn a non-copying or stable string into
evidence of useful authorship. The frozen governor remains a secondary lexical
eligibility check only.

Any second-call challenge or revision route must preserve the earlier matched
pressures: an independent second sample, exact draft replay without challenge,
call-matched generic static guidance, and the proposed challenge. Governor
policy feedback is a distinct causal parent and cannot enter that route
silently or count as environment counterevidence.

## Authority and lineage

The environment owns consequences. The model owns accounts, challenges,
revisions, probe choices, and candidates. The formation runtime owns exact
retention, declared replay, and governor invocation. The trajectory harness
owns forks, assignments, budgets, and evidence capture. A scorer owns only
post-contact verdicts.

Every later model invocation must cite its exact public parents. Another sample,
draft replay, static guidance, governor feedback, and a new environment result
are different causal parents. No mechanism may collapse them into a generic
"reflection" step.

## Loses-conditions

A proposed next route loses before a charter if:

- its prompt, schema, example, runtime transform, or feedback states the
  realized phase-coupled relation;
- it selects a favorable account or candidate after seeing hidden truth;
- it changes experience, representation, call count, feedback, and sampling
  together while naming one cause;
- it makes interesting measurement conditional on governor admission;
- it treats malformed output or token disparity as a research null;
- it treats a repaired interface as lifecycle or developmental progress;
- it treats non-copying text, stability, or governor admission as authorship
  success;
- it omits direct, static, replay, repeated-source, or restatement pressures
  while claiming to eliminate them;
- it hides an unavailable same-call or prompt-mass comparison behind stronger
  causal language; or
- awkward behavior triggers model substitution or another admission loop.

## Completion condition

This boundary is ready for route selection only when two independent reviewers
can reconstruct the separation between research uncertainty and instrument
debt, show that no listed route or candidate vocabulary is already privileged,
and fail to find an answer-shaped or selection-based shortcut in the
comparator pressure.

That review would license only a separate route-selection artifact. It would
not license a mechanism, instrument repair, cases, budget, implementation,
runner, model call, successor contact, or Formation claim.

## Review record

Composer 2.5 and Grok 4.6 first returned `REVISE_PROBLEM`. Both found that the
draft silently privileged production of a stable non-copying candidate, which
collapsed the research target toward the frozen governor. They also found that
the draft omitted World F's stable repeated-occurrence difference, dropped
occurrence repetition from comparator pressure, and treated lexical account
copying as stronger semantic evidence than the `indeterminate` diagnostic
allowed.

The repair asks a route-selection question, treats copying, stability, and
admission only as eligibility or comparison observations, preserves both
accounts as semantically indeterminate, restores repeated-source pressure, and
adds an analytical stop that cannot open a contact staircase. It also carries
forward responsibility ambiguity, matched second-call controls, and the
separate causal parentage of governor feedback.
