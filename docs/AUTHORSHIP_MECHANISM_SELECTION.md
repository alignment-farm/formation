# Experience-grounded authorship mechanism selection

Status: **independently review-stable analytical selection; a separate
occurrence-accounting mechanism proposal is licensed, but no cases, model
calls, budget, protocol, implementation, or charter are licensed**.

## Decision

Select **model-authored occurrence accounting before candidate authorship** for
a separate mechanism proposal.

Hold the exact occurrence, public rule, model, and governor fixed. Instead of
asking one cold call to compress action, consequence, attribution,
applicability, and counterevidence into a candidate, first ask the model for a
plain account of what happened and what remains uncertain. The runtime retains
that exact model-authored account. A later cold call receives the exact
occurrence and exact account and may propose a candidate, decline, or return
nothing.

The account is not new environment evidence. It is model-authored interpretive
work made available to the practitioner. Any later candidate difference remains
compatible with ordinary prompt-content effects. This route is selected because
it changes interpretive process while leaving the developmental occurrence
unchanged.

This decision selects only a route to specify. It does not freeze the account
interface, candidate interface, prompts, branches, scorer, budget, cases, or
contact.

## Why this route

The completed phase-coupled contact already supplied enough public information
to identify the hidden family profile. The public rule stated that the profile
was stable within a family and that control strings did not transfer across
devices. Both acquisition pairs exposed the realized profile. A second
guaranteed-identifying occurrence would therefore add repetition and salience,
not a missing family-binding fact.

The one-shot interpreter call still had several responsibilities at once. It
had to understand the occurrence, separate identifiers from structure, state a
change, limit its scope, and name counterevidence. Both `change` fields copied
control tokens, and World B's `counterevidence` copied the field name
`movement_direction`. This does not prove that compression caused the result.
It makes interpretive decomposition the narrowest live pressure that does not
invent another experience or return a policy lesson.

The selected route is part of the formation loop, not hidden model memory. The
model authors an account; the runtime preserves it with source lineage; another
cold model invocation may use it. The important observation is whether that
model-authored intermediate changes what the practitioner can author next, not
whether an internal state survived between calls.

## Why the rehearsal draft was rejected

An earlier draft selected a second same-family occurrence on new opaque control
tokens. Composer 2.5 and Grok 4.6 both returned `REVISE_SELECTION`.

The public rule plus the first identifying occurrence already supplied the
realized binding, its family scope, and the ban on token reuse. A second
guaranteed-identifying pair would be an empirical restatement of those facts.
Its defensible role would be a salience or repeated-example diagnostic, not a
new developmental source. The draft also mixed another call, draft visibility,
new occurrence text, and new consequence.

That route remains available as a later comparator or separate salience
question. It is not the selected authorship mechanism.

## Routes not selected

### Governor-feedback revision

The exact refusal basis would mostly restate the no-token-reuse rule already
present in the first request. A revision could show response to policy
highlighting, but not that the occurrence grounded the family relation.

### Draft replay or candidate self-critique

Returning the first candidate to another call could change wording, but it
makes a rejected answer the main new input. That risks a screen-and-retry loop
and does not expose where occurrence understanding failed.

### Occurrence-schema repair

Adding slots, phase branches, an inverse, or a more explicit consequence form
could write the missing relation into the interface. The selected route keeps
the reviewed occurrence projection unchanged.

### Model substitution

The operational Qwen artifact participated. Its awkward interpretation is the
phenomenon under study, not a reason to reopen admission.

## Causal shape to preserve

A future mechanism proposal must preserve this authority order:

```text
practice occurrence and external consequence
  -> occurrence account authored by model
  -> exact account retained by formation runtime with source parents
  -> candidate proposed by a later cold model call, or observable decline/null
  -> frozen governor decision recorded after authorship
```

The governor does not stand between account and candidate. It does not score,
repair, summarize, or respond to the account. Candidate eligibility remains a
later and separate event. An awkward, token-bound, empty, or incoherent account
still reaches the candidate responsibility under the predeclared mechanism; it
does not trigger resampling or cancellation.

The account is exact model output retained under its invocation and source
parents. It is not a `candidate proposed` version, not governor-eligible, and
not an environment occurrence. Only the later model-authored object may become
a candidate proposal. The runtime is recorder and causal assembler, not
semantic author. No provider conversation or undeclared session state may
connect the calls.

## Representation boundary

The account responsibility may ask only for a faithful recount of the
occurrence and residual uncertainty. It may not ask for a reusable change,
preparedness, applicability, counterevidence, a phase-to-slot reconstruction,
inverse branches, prospective controls, or extra emphasis on the token-reuse
rule. It may not name the realized family profile or include examples of the
desired abstraction.

The account representation must be capable of retaining awkward natural
language. A shape constraint may enforce only a minimal container and must stay
identical wherever the account responsibility is compared. Shape compliance is
instrumentation, not evidence of understanding.

The candidate responsibility remains generic: propose what, if anything,
should change in preparedness, where it applies, and what later observation
would count against it. A mechanism proposal must show that any revised wording
does not answer the phase-coupled problem on the model's behalf.

## Comparator pressure

A mechanism proposal must distinguish at least these sources of difference:

1. **Direct authorship:** a cold call receives the exact occurrence and generic
   candidate responsibility, with no account.
2. **Independent direct sample:** another cold call receives the same direct
   request. This exposes ordinary sampling variation.
3. **Account generated but withheld:** the account call occurs and is retained,
   but the later candidate request is byte-identical to direct authorship. This
   checks that an undisclosed call or provider state does not carry influence.
4. **Account delivered:** the later candidate request receives the exact
   occurrence plus the exact model-authored account.
5. **Occurrence repeated:** the later candidate request receives the occurrence
   twice under a frozen neutral wrapper but no account. This diagnoses extra
   occurrence bytes and repeated attention.
6. **Static expanded instruction:** one cold candidate call receives the exact
   occurrence plus generic instruction to inspect it before proposing, without
   a persisted model-authored account. This is a static prompting ceiling.
7. **Same-response sequence:** one cold call exposes an account and then a
   candidate in the same response, with no runtime persistence between them.
   A match with account delivery reduces the separate-call route to ordinary
   generated-intermediate prompting plus a recorder.
8. **Deterministic restatement:** the candidate receives a frozen generic
   rendering of the public occurrence fields, with no model author and no
   hidden profile, under the same delivery wrapper. This tests whether a second
   encoding of already-present facts explains the difference.
9. **Account-content control:** from the same exact retained account head, the
   candidate receives a frozen length-matched non-account value under the same
   wrapper. This tests whether extra delivered mass rather than account content
   explains the difference.

These are conceptual pressures, not frozen branches. A proposal must resolve
call count, visible request bytes, draft availability, context length, sampling,
and inference settings without claiming that impossible byte parity exists
between semantically different inputs.

One exact account must be authored and retained before the proposal forks the
account-withheld, account-delivered, and account-content-control paths. Their
candidate calls must share the frozen shell and independently sample from the
same model configuration. The delivery wrapper must be identical between
account, restatement, and content-control material. Direct, repeated-occurrence,
static-expanded, and same-response calls never receive the retained account.

The account-withheld candidate request must be byte-identical to direct
authorship. A single stochastic action or text difference between those cells
is sampling, not evidence that an undisclosed call carried influence. Any
request, provider-session, or configuration mismatch invalidates the cold-call
comparison.

A proposal must include symmetric predeclared sampling for direct and
account-delivered requests; it may not compare repeatability on one side with a
single draw on the other. It must report prompt length and natural call cost.

An account-delivered candidate difference is only `account-associated` unless
stronger prospective evidence later earns stronger language. If same-response
sequencing matches it, generated-intermediate prompting remains an adequate
explanation. If deterministic restatement matches it, a second encoding of the
occurrence may explain the result. If the content control matches it, delivered
mass may explain the result. Static-expanded, occurrence-repeated, and direct
sampling collapses remain equally live.

## Primary observables

The selected mechanism ends at candidate authorship. A future exploratory
record may report:

- exact account and candidate contents, including nulls and refusals;
- exact source and request-parent lineage;
- token or occurrence-field copying under predeclared lexical diagnostics;
- scorer-owned semantic classifications kept outside runtime lineage;
- candidate eligibility as a separate governance observation; and
- differences and collapses across the predeclared authorship comparators.

Admissibility is not success. Later action is not required to observe the
authorship mechanism. A later influence contact, if ever proposed, must remain
a separate boundary.

## Scorer boundary

A post-contact scorer may use the hidden profile to classify whether an exact
account or candidate is correct, incorrect, absent, token-bound, overly broad,
or non-falsifiable. It may not author a summary, choose a candidate, affect
admission, or return feedback to the practitioner.

Natural-language semantics may not support a reliable automated verdict. If a
pre-contact scorer cannot be frozen and independently challenged, the contact
must retain exact texts and use only descriptive or lexical classifications.
A scorer label is never acquired competence.

## Meaningful weak and null results

This route remains informative without a positive candidate:

- If accounts and candidates keep copying episode tokens, interpretive
  decomposition did not repair token binding in this contact.
- If direct samples vary as much as account-delivered samples, sampling remains
  an adequate explanation.
- If occurrence repetition matches account delivery, repeated source salience
  remains an adequate explanation.
- If static expanded instruction matches account delivery, a persisted
  model-authored intermediate has not beaten ordinary static prompting.
- If same-response sequencing matches account delivery, the separate runtime
  object has not beaten ordinary generated-intermediate prompting.
- If deterministic restatement matches account delivery, re-encoding the
  already-present occurrence remains an adequate explanation.
- If the length-matched content control matches account delivery, extra prompt
  mass remains an adequate explanation.
- If a supposedly cold withheld request differs in bytes, provider state, or
  configuration from direct authorship, the comparison is invalid. An isolated
  output difference between byte-identical cold requests is only sampling.
- If account delivery changes candidates but semantic status remains wrong or
  indeterminate, the mechanism changed text without earning useful authorship.

Every one of these outcomes closes a bounded exploration. None licenses
governor feedback, rehearsal, schema repair, model search, or repeated sampling.

## Design loses-conditions

The mechanism proposal fails before a charter if:

- the account prompt or schema names the missing family relation;
- the runtime or harness summarizes, filters, repairs, or selects the account;
- the account reaches the candidate only after an admissibility or convenience
  test;
- the account and candidate are produced in one provider conversation or share
  undeclared state;
- comparison requests carry branch, family-profile, expected-action, score, or
  verdict labels;
- a semantic scorer influences runtime lineage or candidate choice;
- later admission or action is required for authorship to count as observable;
  or
- a null result triggers prompt repair, resampling, model selection, or another
  interface staircase.

## Review question

Independent reviewers must try to show that the selected route merely adds
chain-of-thought-like prompt content, that the account responsibility supplies
the abstraction, that the comparators cannot distinguish sampling and repeated
source text, or that the runtime becomes the candidate's semantic author.

Two stable reviews would license only a separate mechanism proposal that freezes
the account responsibility and comparator semantics. They would not license
identifiers, cases, a protocol, a budget, implementation, a runner, model calls,
or a Formation finding.

## Review record

The first reviewed draft selected cross-token rehearsal. Composer 2.5 and Grok
4.6 both returned `REVISE_SELECTION`: the public rule plus the first acquisition
already supplied the family binding, family scope, and token non-transfer rule,
so another guaranteed-identifying occurrence would mainly add salience.

The rewritten draft selected model-authored occurrence accounting. Grok then
returned `REVISE_SELECTION` because account delivery could still collapse to
generated intermediate reasoning or extra prompt mass; Composer returned
`SELECTION_STABLE` while recommending related proposal-level controls. The
repair added same-response sequencing, deterministic restatement,
length-matched non-account delivery, shared-account fork discipline, symmetric
sampling, explicit record binding, and their null interpretations.

Final read-only Cursor verdicts:

- `composer-2.5`: `SELECTION_STABLE`
- `cursor-grok-4.6-high-fast`: `SELECTION_STABLE`

These verdicts license only a separate mechanism proposal.
