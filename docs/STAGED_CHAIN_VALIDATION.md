# Prospective validation of the staged observation chain

Status: **frozen before contact under the session-wide human authorization**.

## Question

Across four fresh source families, does an environment consequence cause a
model-written observation and table that improve later matching action while a
family gate protects unrelated action?

## Comparison

Each source family begins with a target one step lower, prospectively stressing
the second-slot consequence that direct authorship repeatedly mishandled. The
model acts once. One observation author sees the environment result. A second
sees the same action with the result withheld. Separate cold calls turn each
observation into an effect table. A direct author also receives the raw action
and result.

Each source then receives four fresh later cases: two matching-family devices,
one target above and one below, and two opposite-rule nonmatching devices, one
target above and one below. Every branch and case receives four identical
calls.

The eight later branches are cold, raw experience, direct authored table,
consequence-exposed staged table with family gating, consequence-withheld staged
table with family gating, removal of the exposed staged table, exposed staged
table without gating, and a supplied correct table with gating.

The fixed schedule contains 536 calls: 24 acquisition and authorship calls and
512 later actions. The physical ceiling is 544 attempts with at most eight
transport retries. The exact Qwen artifact and Docker interface remain
unchanged. Evidence is retained under `evidence/staged-chain-validation-<run-id>/`
and replayed before exit.

## Frozen verdict

The validation is `not_engaged` unless all exposed direct observations and
staged tables are exact and the supplied table scores at least 14 of 16 in each
matching direction.

It is `harmful` if gated staged delivery loses at least four unrelated actions
to cold action.

It is `supported` only if all of these conditions hold:

- gated staged delivery scores at least 28 of 32 matching actions;
- it scores at least 14 of 16 in each movement direction;
- it beats cold, direct authorship, consequence-withheld staged authorship, and
  delivery removal by at least 24 matching actions each;
- it beats raw experience by at least 16 matching actions;
- it loses no more than two unrelated actions to cold;
- gating prevents at least eight errors caused by ungated delivery; and
- every branch-case contains at least three valid actions.

Otherwise the verdict is null. `Supported` would validate this bounded staged
mechanism against its frozen comparisons. Formation would remain null because
revision, longer accumulation, and another domain are not tested.
