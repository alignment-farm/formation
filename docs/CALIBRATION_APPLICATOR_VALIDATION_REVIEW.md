# Calibration-applicator validation peer review

Status: **two-model review complete; both verdicts require revision; the draft
does not license implementation or contact**.

## Review setup

On 2026-08-18, two independent Cursor CLI sessions reviewed the prospective
[validation draft](CALIBRATION_APPLICATOR_VALIDATION.md) in read-only ask mode:

- `composer-2.5`; and
- `cursor-grok-4.6-high`.

Both received the same repository authority packet and the same instruction to
attack applicator tautology, baseline weakness, maintenance advantage, schema
leakage, ablation overclaim, cost masking, schedule arithmetic, branch identity,
information flow, verdict completeness, and replication. Neither saw the
other's response. Both returned `REVISE_BEFORE_BUILD`.

Agreement is not treated as proof. The reviews matter because they supplied
concrete counterexamples that can satisfy the draft's verdict without
establishing its stated comparison.

## Shared blocking result

Once a correct family slot enters the deterministic applicator, both movement
directions are correct by construction. Cold and ablation branches request the
same missing fact and can still reach the target one action later. The proposed
positive thresholds therefore measure fact presence, caching, and a working
applicator edge. They do not make later model-mediated competence risky.

Both reviewers also found that the draft could return `supported` without a
favorable natural-cost result over raw persistence. Reporting a cost vector is
not enough when no frozen verdict rule lets a raw or authored win defeat the
claim.

The ablation remains useful, but only as an `edge_functional` wiring result. It
cannot carry a developmental verdict.

## Additional blocking counterexamples

The reviews identified these independent failures:

- A safe acquisition-time `request_calibration` places `increasing_slot`
  directly in the consequence under the same name the fact author must emit.
  That path tests transcription rather than interpretation.
- Proposed device names (`rise`, `fall`, `other`, `current`, and `maint`), token
  prefixes, and the derived `-x` family leak prospective episode class into
  public foreground.
- Raw direct practice is told to request calibration unless a parsed fact is
  present, even though its declared evidence is a raw occurrence. The baseline
  can therefore collapse into cold behavior without using its occurrence.
- The ungoverned applicator branch is structurally unable to react to the
  public maintenance notice, while authored direct practice can. A governance
  win against that branch is designed into the API.
- The engagement floor of six correct admitted facts also supplies the exact
  twelve positive cells needed to pass the first transfer threshold. Engagement
  and claimed effect are therefore not independent pressures.
- Eight single draws at temperature 0.6 do not justify a stable comparative
  claim, and the conditional schedule leaves call ownership and some ordering
  details underspecified.

Composer additionally noted that admission failure was absorbed into
`not_engaged`, weakening the prospective test of governance. Grok additionally
identified ambiguous index bases and trajectory-versus-episode nesting in the
rotation rule. The maximum-call arithmetic itself was correct:
`2 + 8 + 8 + 40 + 40 + 40 = 138`, beneath the proposed 142-attempt ceiling.

## Decision

Do not repair this packet by adding thresholds, repetitions, and branches until
it passes. Those changes cannot remove its central identifying problem: the
runtime executes the acquired relation, so later success follows mechanically
from a correct fact.

The [calibration-fact applicator](CALIBRATION_APPLICATOR_MECHANISM.md) remains a
coherent practitioner architecture and a possible diagnostic of fact caching,
scope, suspension, and cost. It is closed as the next claim-bearing Formation
mechanism. Its uncontacted prospective cases remain unconsumed but unlicensed.

The next problem returns the later action to the cold model. Formation must ask
whether a model-authored change can influence both sides of a later conditional
decision without a runtime applicator or answer-shaped candidate schema
executing that change on the model's behalf.
