# Unselected lineage charter decision

Status: **independently review-stable decision to draft one strict-budget
exploratory charter; no runner, participant-model request, validation packet,
or Formation claim is licensed by this decision**.

## Decision

Draft one exploratory charter for the reviewed
[mechanism](UNSELECTED_LINEAGE_BEHAVIOR_MECHANISM.md) and conformant
[specimen](UNSELECTED_LINEAGE_BEHAVIOR_SPECIMEN.md).

The deterministic work has closed the questions it was allowed to answer. The
environment applies only committed actions. All case roles are prospective.
The six information paths are distinct. Every assigned history can reach later
behavior. The scorer keeps a complete denominator. Another deterministic layer
would not resolve the remaining uncertainty, which is model behavior under
those paths.

## Operational choices for the charter

Use the same exact Qwen3 14B Docker artifact as the completed contact. This is
operational continuity, not admission or a claim that the model occupies an
ideal developmental band. Do not open a model search.

Use four fresh live blocks. Four is the minimum that fully crosses both hidden
profiles with both acquisition target directions while giving every branch and
case role a denominator of four. This remains a small exploratory sample. It
cannot support a population estimate or validation claim.

Each block contains:

- one shared acquisition call;
- one result-withheld and one result-exposed authorship call; and
- six branches by four later behavioral roles.

With one disposable action-interface call, the planned packet therefore has
109 logical calls. A later charter must freeze the exact physical contingency
ceiling, token ceilings, call order, and stopping rule. It may not increase the
block count after seeing variation.

## What the charter must freeze

Before implementation, the charter must bind:

- the exact model artifact, digest, provider, backend, endpoint, and chat
  template;
- four fresh families assigned across the two profile values, plus fresh
  identifiers, devices, controls, states, targets, case roles, branch order,
  and presentation order;
- the complete pre-contact leakage witness and canonical manifest hash;
- exact disposable, acquisition, authorship, and later-action messages;
- the withheld sentinel, raw receipt, static lesson, and generic foreground
  serialization;
- the one shared result-exposed intermediate used by delivery and ablation;
- scalar-content handling, action parsing, provider failure, retry, and total
  unavailable continuation;
- every request-equivalence claim and residual byte and token delta;
- full branch-by-role and acquisition-status denominators;
- completion and physical-call ceilings; and
- null terminal Formation and validation verdicts.

The charter must use fresh live cases rather than the deterministic specimen
identifiers. It must reproduce the specimen obligations with those new cases
before any participant request.

## Claim ceiling and stop

The packet is exploratory. Its strongest allowed output is a bounded
branch-by-role behavioral observation plus intermediate diagnostics. Four
blocks cannot establish durability, revision, net value, population-level
reliability, or a full Formation effect.

Wrong acquisition, empty or unavailable authorship, invalid later action,
static dominance, raw dominance, branch equality, prompt sensitivity, and high
variance all complete the packet. None triggers a prompt repair, extra block,
model substitution, rerun, or successor.

The charter must retain exactly:

```json
{"formation_verdict":null,"validation_verdict":null}
```
