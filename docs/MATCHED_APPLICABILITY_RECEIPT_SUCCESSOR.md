## Frozen verdict

The apparatus is engaged only if supplied receipts complete at least 16 of 18
known-signal tasks, at least eight of nine in each profile, and both exact
full-catalog and silent no-record controls attempt at least four of six
unfamiliar-signal tasks. Every learned and supplied receipt cell must contain
at least two available actions across its three calls.

The matched receipt is `supported` only if:

- both exact predecessor packets are bound with their unchanged `harmful` and
  `not_engaged` verdicts;
- both retained records remain admitted, exact, and opposite;
- all eight diagnostic results match the harmful predecessor;
- all 72 reconstructed full-catalog and no-record control requests match their
  exact parent request hashes;
- all 144 assignments match exact signal comparison;
- all 24 learned and supplied receipt requests are byte-identical;
- all six unfamiliar-signal learned, supplied, and reversed receipt groups are
  byte-identical;
- learned receipts complete at least 16 of 18 known-signal tasks, at least
  eight of nine in each known profile, with no more than one failure;
- learned receipt completion exceeds the no-record control by at least six and
  trails supplied receipt completion by no more than two;
- reversed receipts cause at least 14 known-signal failures;
- learned empty receipts cause at most one unfamiliar-signal task attempt and
  at most one failure; and
- they cause at least four fewer unfamiliar-signal task attempts than both the
  exact full-catalog and exact silent no-record controls.

If the apparatus is engaged but learned empty receipts cause at least three
unfamiliar-signal task attempts or failures, the result is `harmful`.
Otherwise it is `supported` or `null`.

result is `not_engaged`. That would show that the no-match behavior is not yet
stable enough to validate a receipt mechanism.
