# Model-mediated procedural influence problem

Status: **closed at mechanism and deterministic-specimen scope by the reviewed
phase-coupled profile-influence route; no exploratory charter, validation
packet, or model contact licensed**.

## Problem

The explicit-consequence contact produced a correct, admitted fact that changed
one later action direction but not its logical complement. A deterministic
applicator could force both directions to work, but independent
[peer review](CALIBRATION_APPLICATOR_VALIDATION_REVIEW.md) showed that such a
comparison would mainly validate stored fact presence and runtime execution.

The next question is narrower than Formation but still behaviorally risky:

> Can a model-authored interpretation of consequence alter the cold model's
> later handling of a conditional relation in all warranted directions, without
> the runtime, schema, or current foreground executing that relation for it?

The runtime may preserve, govern, deliver, and ablate a candidate. The later
model must remain responsible for turning it into an action.

## What is now closed

- Do not reopen model admission or search for a more convenient practitioner.
- Do not add another explicit consequence field, inferred inverse, action
  table, or deterministic action applicator to the calibration packet.
- Do not promote the rejected applicator draft by increasing its sample or
  tightening thresholds; its identifying contrast is wrong before statistics.
- Do not treat delivery, candidate presence, or an applicator-edge receipt as
  attributable model influence.

The applicator design is retained as a diagnostic and engineering option. It is
not negative evidence about the utility of situated memory; it is negative
design evidence about what this project can call developmental validation.

## Representation pressure

A next candidate must be authored from the occurrence and consequence without
an answer-shaped form. In particular, its schema or prompt may identify a
generic responsibility such as “state the reusable change,” but may not provide
the branches, inverse, action table, or prospective control tokens that the
model is supposed to discover and later use.

Natural language is not automatically safe. A frozen template that says “when
the target is above do X; when below do Y” already supplies the procedure even
if the model fills two blanks. A generic program or policy language has the same
problem if the runtime executes it and the claimed outcome is that execution.

The representation must therefore be judged by what competence remains at the
later model boundary, not by whether the candidate was technically model text.

## Minimum comparison pressure

Before fresh validation cases are frozen, a mechanism proposal must make these
contrasts possible:

1. The same cold model and action interface act on identical current
   foreground across branches.
2. Raw direct practice receives the exact occurrence in its ordinary usable
   form, without being forced through a treatment-specific extractor.
3. Authored and governed branches receive the same exact candidate when the
   governor permits it; governance cannot repair or expand the candidate.
4. Both complementary directions require later model judgment with fresh,
   opaque action tokens.
5. A non-transfer case is more lexically similar than at least one true
   transfer case, so family or template matching cannot win by itself.
6. Current-state-sufficient cases can expose unnecessary use.
7. A public counterevidence event is visible to direct authored practice as
   well as the governor. Governance must beat or cost less than that strong
   comparator; it cannot win by withholding public context.
8. A candidate-presence ablation changes only the offered candidate. Its result
   is reported separately from benefit and transfer.
9. Natural cost has frozen dominance rules: a raw or authored accuracy or
   safety win can defeat support rather than merely appear in a table.

## Calibration may not be the final domain

The two-slot world remains useful because its hidden fact and consequences are
exact. It may be too small to distinguish procedural formation from a salient
fact cue: the entire reusable content can fit in one slot value.

The next analytical choice is therefore between:

- a calibration successor in which the experience supports a relation that
  cannot be reduced to one positively named action cue; or
- another cheap deterministic practice where consequence can support a
  reusable check or strategy and prospective cases change surface form.

This is domain selection for identifying leverage, not model selection. A new
domain must still preserve same-model baselines, external consequence,
prospective transfer and non-transfer, and strict separation between runtime
and trajectory harness.

## Exit condition

This problem closes only when a proposed experience, candidate representation,
later practice boundary, raw baseline, governance pressure, and ablation leave
the acquired conditional competence genuinely at risk in the later cold-model
action. Two independent read-only reviews must be able to state a concrete way
the mechanism could lose without relying on malformed output or infrastructure
failure.

The reviewed [phase-coupled proposal](PHASE_COUPLED_CONTROL_PROPOSAL.md) meets
this exit at its deliberately narrow one-bit ceiling, and the
[deterministic specimen](PHASE_COUPLED_CONTROL_SPECIMEN.md) establishes its
pre-contact obligations. Fresh cases, thresholds, and a model-call budget
remain unselected until a separate exploratory charter is reviewed.
