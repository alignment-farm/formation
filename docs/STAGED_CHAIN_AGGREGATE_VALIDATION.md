## Comparison

Eight fresh source families are fixed before contact. Four have a first action
that increases position. Four have a second action that increases position.
Every acquisition target is one step below its start. The environment applies
the model's selected action and issues the selected slot and actual movement.

For each source, one observation author sees that result. Another sees the same
occurrence with the result withheld. Separate cold calls turn both observations
into complete effect tables. A direct author receives the raw occurrence and
result without the observation step.

Each source then receives four new cases. Two use the same controller family,
with one target above and one below. Two use a new family with the opposite
relation, again with one target above and one below. Every later branch and case
receives three identical calls.

The eight later branches are no retained information, raw experience, the
direct table with a family check, the consequence-exposed staged table with the
same check, the consequence-hidden staged table with the check, removal of the
exposed table, the exposed table without the check, and a supplied exact table
with the check.

The harness may apply external results, preserve exact model text, compare
exact family identity, and score actions. It may not write or repair an
observation or table, choose an action from retained material, or resample a
valid model output.

## Frozen verdict

The validation is `not_engaged` unless at least seven of eight exposed
observations and staged tables are exact. The supplied exact table must also
make at least 43 of 48 matching actions, at least 21 of 24 in each movement
direction, and at least 21 of 24 in each true-relation group.

It is `harmful` if the family-checked exposed table loses at least six unrelated
actions compared with no retained information.

It is `supported` only if all of these conditions hold:

- the family-checked exposed table makes at least 43 of 48 matching actions;
- it makes at least 21 of 24 in each movement direction and each true-relation
  group;
- it makes at least 9 of 12 in each relation-by-direction quadrant;
- it beats no retained information, raw experience, and table removal by at
  least 16 matching actions each;
- it beats direct and consequence-hidden authorship by at least 12 matching
  actions each;
- it trails the supplied exact table by no more than three matching actions;
- it loses no more than three unrelated actions compared with no retained
  information; and
- every three-call branch and case contains at least two valid action objects.

The scorer reports how many unrelated errors the family check prevented, but
that number cannot help the verdict. A representation that causes less harm
without the check is not penalized.

Otherwise the verdict is null. A supported result would validate this bounded
staged mechanism against the frozen causal controls. Formation would remain
null because the mechanism is not better than a supplied exact lesson and the
packet does not test revision, accumulation, or another domain.
