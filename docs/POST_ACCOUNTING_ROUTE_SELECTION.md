# Post-accounting authorship route selection

Status: **independently review-stable analytical selection; a separate
challenge-and-revision mechanism proposal is licensed, but no prompts,
interfaces, instrument changes, cases, budget, implementation, runner, or model
call is licensed**.

## Decision

Select **unconditional model-authored challenge of an exact first draft before
governance** for a separate mechanism proposal.

A cold model first authors one candidate from the exact occurrence and public
rule. The runtime retains the complete model output without scoring or repair.
A later cold model invocation may receive that exact draft plus a generic
epistemic challenge: inspect whether the draft is supported, retain it, revise
it, withdraw it, or remain uncertain. The later output is model-authored. The
governor acts only after the challenge path finishes.

Every observable first draft enters the selected path. Token copying,
incorrectness, awkwardness, null fields, or likely rejection does not cancel
the challenge or trigger a replacement sample. Malformed JSON that still
exists as exact string content remains raw draft text and enters the challenge
without repair, completion, or parsing claims. An absent or non-string provider
content value makes only the draft-dependent path unavailable.

This route changes interpretive process, not experience. It supplies no new
environment result, realized relation, expected answer, scorer label, or
governor feedback. It is selected for specification because it lets the model
encounter and respond to its own awkward authorship without requiring that the
first draft be experimentally convenient.

The selection does not claim that challenge produces a better candidate. A
revision may be a sampling difference, response to static guidance, response
to extra draft bytes, same-call reflection, or a worse answer.

## Why this route is live

The occurrence-accounting contact found stable token-shaped direct candidates
in both worlds before any account was generated. Occurrence accounts did not
isolate a delivery effect, and deterministic restatement also produced varied
relational prose. The record therefore does not justify another account prompt
or a claim that persistence caused token attraction.

It does show one exact, repeatable kind of awkward first draft. Treating that
draft as developmental material is different from screening it out. A challenge
path can observe whether the practitioner retains, changes, or withdraws its
own proposal while the original experience stays fixed.

This is not yet experience-grounded revision. The draft and generic challenge
are additional prompt content. A later mechanism must preserve that weak claim
ceiling and expose the corresponding controls.

## Why the other routes are not selected

### Responsibility-legibility prompt

The existing responsibility already asked for a reusable change, scope, and
counterevidence. Rewording it after stable token-shaped outputs would be direct
prompt repair. A generic challenge still changes instruction, but its exact
first-draft parent makes revision and retention observable rather than silently
replacing the original responsibility.

### Repeated-source salience

World F already produced a stable repeated-occurrence difference consisting of
the copied field name `movement_direction`; World E was unstable. Repeating the
occurrence again would study salience or field copying, not a missing
developmental fact. Occurrence repetition remains a required control.

### Model-chosen additional experience

The first phase-coupled occurrence already contained enough public information
to identify its family profile. A model-chosen probe could study active
practice, but it would add probe selection, a new action, a new consequence,
and another occurrence. That is a broader experience-history intervention than
the selected authorship route.

### Immediate analytical stop

An unconditional challenge can be specified without returning the governor's
answer or changing the occurrence. The route therefore survives the current
analytical stop test. If independent review shows that its controls cannot
separate draft presence, another call, static challenge, and same-response
reflection, the selection closes without a mechanism proposal or contact.

## Causal order to preserve

```text
exact practice occurrence
  -> first candidate attempt authored by cold model
  -> exact first output retained without semantic selection
  -> unconditional challenge invocation by later cold model
  -> retained, revised, withdrawn, null, malformed, or uncertain output
  -> frozen governor observation, if a parseable candidate exists
```

The first output is a model-invocation result and, when parseable, a candidate
proposal. The challenge does not erase it. A later parseable proposal is a new
candidate version with the first draft and challenge configuration as causal
parents. Withdrawal or no proposal is also observable. Neither event is a new
practice occurrence.

The governor has no causal path into the challenge request. Its exact policy
rejection, expected eligibility, and token-copy diagnosis remain harness or
post-authorship information until governance actually occurs. A future
mechanism may not pre-score the first draft to decide whether challenge is
worth running.

## Challenge responsibility boundary

The generic challenge may ask the model to inspect support, uncertainty,
scope, and counterevidence in its exact prior draft. It may not mention:

- acquisition control-token reuse;
- episode identifiers versus reusable roles;
- ordered slots, phase-to-slot mapping, inverse profiles, or the realized
  family relation;
- the expected candidate, action, eligibility result, or scorer verdict;
- a governor rule or rejection code; or
- examples of a corrected answer.

This restriction keeps the desired phase-coupled abstraction at risk. It also
means the selected route may fail to make the developmental responsibility
legible. That is a valid null, not permission to add hints.

## Comparator pressure

A mechanism proposal must separate at least these sources of later candidate
difference:

1. **Independent direct sample.** The exact original candidate request runs
   again with no first draft or challenge.
2. **Draft generated but withheld.** The first draft invocation exists in
   lineage, but the later request is byte-identical to independent direct. This
   audits hidden provider carryover and sampling.
3. **Exact draft replay.** The exact first output is delivered under the
   unchanged base candidate responsibility, without challenge language. This
   isolates draft presence and extra content.
4. **Static challenge.** Generic challenge guidance is present without the
   first draft. This is a disclosed non-parity static ceiling with non-indexical
   wording; it cannot say "inspect the prior draft" when no draft exists.
5. **Draft plus challenge.** The selected interaction receives the exact first
   output and exact generic challenge.
6. **Same-response draft and challenge.** One cold call authors a first draft
   and then challenges it in one response, with no runtime retention between
   them. This is the generated-intermediate explanation for a separate runtime
   object.
7. **Occurrence repeated.** The exact occurrence is repeated without a new
   environment event, first draft, or challenge.
8. **Deterministic restatement.** A model-free rendering of the same occurrence
   facts is delivered without first-draft authorship.
9. **Prompt-mass control.** A predeclared non-draft value tests extra visible
   mass without deriving favorable semantic content from the live draft.

These are pressures for a later proposal, not frozen branches or prompts. The
same number of independent samples and the same inference settings must apply
to every claim-bearing condition. Another call, draft visibility, challenge
language, response sequencing, repeated source, restatement, prompt mass, and
sampling may not change together under one causal name.

The earlier exact-byte mass control failed token parity. A proposal may either
freeze a new token-aware instrument before contact or keep prompt mass as an
explicit live explanation and use weaker language. Repairing the control does
not count as authorship progress.

The earlier same-response condition frequently hit its completion ceiling. A
proposal claiming separate runtime retention must make the same-response
burden observable under a predeclared symmetric budget. If it cannot, the
retention-specific claim is unavailable. Extra completion budget is
instrumentation and applies symmetrically to comparable multi-stage outputs.

## What could be observed

A mechanism may observe exact first and later outputs, including malformed,
null, retained, revised, or withdrawn results. It may compare exact candidate
bytes and separately apply a frozen post-contact semantic scorer if one can be
reviewed without answer leakage.

Exact change is not improvement. Non-copying is not useful authorship.
Governor admission is not developmental success. A later route-specific label
could be no stronger than `draft-challenge-associated candidate revision`, and
only after independent sampling, withheld, replay, static challenge,
same-response, occurrence-repetition, deterministic-restatement, and prompt-
mass conditions are all available and satisfy their predeclared stability and
validity rules. If any is unavailable or unstable, only per-condition
observations are permitted and its explanation remains live. Even the complete
label would describe frozen request conditions, not acquired competence or
runtime retention.

The following are valid terminal results:

- exact retention of the first draft;
- unstable or malformed revision;
- withdrawal or null;
- a revision reproduced by independent sampling;
- a revision reproduced by draft replay alone;
- a revision reproduced by static challenge;
- a revision reproduced within one response;
- a difference left confounded by occurrence repetition, restatement, or
  prompt mass;
- a parseable but wrong or token-bound revision; or
- no available challenge comparison.

One bounded null must close the selected route. It may not trigger a sequence
through responsibility repair, added experience, model substitution, or a new
challenge prompt.

## Authority and scorer boundary

The model owns both draft versions, challenge responses, withdrawals, and
uncertainty. The runtime owns exact retention and declared replay. The governor
owns only post-authorship eligibility. The harness owns forks, comparator
assignment, budgets, and evidence. A scorer owns only post-contact semantic
classification.

No runtime or harness component may summarize the draft before replay, select a
favorable draft, translate tokens into roles, or make a challenge conditional
on hidden truth. A scorer may not write runtime lineage, choose a revision, or
feed back a verdict.

## Loses-conditions

The selection fails before a mechanism proposal if:

- challenge wording names the missing relation or token-reuse repair;
- only rejected, token-bound, or otherwise convenient drafts are challenged;
- a malformed or awkward first draft is replaced by another sample;
- governor feedback enters the challenge path;
- draft replay, static challenge, and another call are not separated;
- occurrence repetition, deterministic restatement, or prompt mass is omitted
  while stronger causal language is retained;
- same-response unavailability is hidden while runtime retention is claimed;
- stability, non-copying, revision, or admission is scored as useful
  authorship;
- instrument repair is counted as research or lifecycle progress; or
- a null opens another prompt, model, route, or admission staircase.

## Review question

Independent reviewers must try to reduce the selected route to another sample,
screen-and-retry, draft priming, static self-critique, generated intermediate
reasoning, repeated source, model-free restatement, prompt mass, or hidden
governor feedback. They must also test whether unconditional challenge really
allows awkward first drafts to remain developmental material rather than an
admission gate.

Two stable reviews would license only a separate mechanism proposal. They
would not license prompts, interfaces, instrument changes, cases, budgets,
implementation, a runner, model contact, successor protocol, or Formation
claim.
