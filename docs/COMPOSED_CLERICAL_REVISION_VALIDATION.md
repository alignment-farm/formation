# Composed clerical revision validation

Status: **frozen before contact under the session-wide human authorization**.

## Question

On four fresh developmental lineages, can the learned clerk write a revised
record, can the composed admission mechanism accept only source-supported
versions, and can admitted revisions guide later cold participant action
without affecting unrelated devices?

This is the first fresh behavioral test of the composed admission gate. It does
not reuse model outputs from the earlier revision or projection contacts.

## Fresh trajectory

Each lineage contains two visible designs with opposite control relations. An
old source occurrence produces a clerk-written record for each design. The old
records first act on new controls above and below the current position.

The environment then reverses all eight relations. A participant acts from the
old record, and the resulting movement contradicts it. Exposed and movement-
hidden clerk chains each transcribe the new source, state both effects, and
parse the prose into named fields.

A separate projection call receives the transcribed actuator and a proposed
record. It copies the proposed effect for that actuator. The runtime applies
the frozen composed checks: source completeness, exact sensory copying, record
shape, exact projection, and equality between the projected claim and the
transcribed observed effect.

Every proposal is retained. Admitted revisions may become version 2. Rejected
stale or hidden proposals remain quarantined and leave version 1 current.

## Comparisons

Before reversal, each old record receives three action calls in both target
directions.

After reversal, each lineage has A above, A below, B above, B below, a novel
design, and a recombination of the two known designs. Every case receives three
calls under eight conditions:

- cold;
- raw counterexperience;
- newest revision after composed admission;
- a stale proposal subjected to the same admission checks;
- a movement-hidden proposal subjected to admission;
- the admitted revision removed, leaving the old version current;
- a supplied correct new sentence selected by normalized visible scope; and
- the clerk-written revision selected by the environment-owned exact scope.

Novel and recombined cases should select no record. Raw reports, rejected
proposals, all old and new versions, and every admission reason remain in
evidence.

## Frozen verdict

The apparatus is not engaged unless supplied new guidance makes at least 43 of
48 matching actions, at least 21 of 24 in each design and target direction, and
every three-call participant cell contains at least two valid actions.

The composed mechanism is supported only if:

- at least seven of eight old records are exact and make at least 43 of 48
  pre-change actions;
- all eight counteractions follow the old policy into a contradictory result;
- at least seven of eight exposed revised records and selected-effect
  projections are exact;
- all eight old records and all eight exposed revisions are admitted;
- all eight stale proposals and all eight movement-hidden proposals are
  quarantined;
- at least 20 of 24 later normalizations and structural selections are exact,
  with no more than two false novel or recombined selections;
- admitted revisions make at least 43 of 48 matching actions and at least 21
  of 24 in each design and target direction;
- admitted revisions exceed cold, raw, stale, hidden, and removal by at least
  16 matching actions each;
- admitted revisions trail supplied guidance by no more than four actions;
- environment-selected clerk revisions make at least 43 matching actions; and
- admitted revisions lose no more than three unrelated actions relative to
  cold.

If the apparatus is engaged but admitted revisions lose at least six unrelated
actions, the verdict is `harmful`. Otherwise it is `supported` or `null`.

This validation can support the bounded composed revision mechanism. It cannot
establish Formation, superiority over supplied guidance, long-horizon
development, or net practical value.
