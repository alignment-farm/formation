# Aggregate validation of staged-table revision

Status: **frozen before contact under the session-wide human authorization**.

## Question

Across eight balanced two-phase worlds, can a new environment consequence cause
the model to replace a previously useful table and improve later action under a
changed relation?

The four-world exploration found exact revisions and strong post-change action
but returned null when one upward cell failed. The supplied exact table failed
the same cell. This validation uses fresh worlds and a frozen aggregate scorer
that can tolerate one completely failed cell without hiding broader failure.

## Comparison

Eight fresh controller families are fixed before contact. Four begin with a
first action that increases position. Four begin with a second action that
increases position. Each family produces one environment result, one
model-written observation, and one model-written effect table.

Before any change, the old table and a cold comparison act three times on new
targets above and below. The old table must work before revision can engage.

The environment then reverses the relation for the same family. A new device
receives the old table. The model acts once and the changed environment issues
the selected slot and actual movement. One observation author sees that result;
another sees the same occurrence with the result hidden. Revision authors
receive the exact old table plus one of those observations and return one
complete current table.

Post-change action uses new matching devices above and below and new unrelated
families above and below. The eight branches are cold, stale old table, old
table plus raw counterexperience, exposed revised table, hidden-consequence
revised table, removal of the exposed revision, a supplied exact new table, and
the exposed revision without the family check. Every branch and case receives
three identical calls.

The stale and removal requests are byte-identical within each world and case.
The harness preserves both table versions and exact developmental lineage. It
does not write a relation, choose an action from a table, or resample a valid
model output.

## Frozen verdict

The validation is `not_engaged` unless at least seven of eight initial
observations, initial tables, exposed counter-observations, and exposed
revisions are exact; the old tables make at least 43 of 48 pre-change actions
and at least 21 of 24 in each direction; at least seven of eight
counterexperience actions follow the old table; and the supplied new tables
make at least 43 of 48 post-change matching actions, at least 21 of 24 in each
direction, and at least 21 of 24 in each changed-relation group.

It is `harmful` if the family-checked exposed revision loses at least six
unrelated post-change actions compared with cold action.

It is `supported` only if:

- the exposed revised table makes at least 43 of 48 post-change matching
  actions;
- it makes at least 21 of 24 in each movement direction and each changed-
  relation group;
- it makes at least 9 of 12 in each relation-by-direction quadrant;
- it beats cold action, the stale old table, and revision removal by at least 16
  matching actions each;
- it beats old table plus raw counterexperience and the hidden-consequence
  revision by at least 12 matching actions each;
- it trails the supplied exact new table by no more than three matching
  actions;
- it loses no more than three unrelated actions compared with cold action; and
- every three-call post-change branch and case contains at least two valid
  action objects.

Gate benefit is reported but cannot help the verdict. Otherwise the verdict is
null. A supported result would validate one bounded revision mechanism in this
domain. Formation would remain null because the mechanism is not better than a
supplied exact update and does not cover accumulation, long delays, another
domain, or net operating value.
