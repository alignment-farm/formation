# Research and engineering plan

Status: **active plan; the 109-call unselected-lineage contact is complete; its

## Program rule

Build only enough machinery to expose the next uncertainty. Separate wire
claims, mechanism claims, and developmental findings. A working runtime does not
establish formation; an attractive trajectory does not establish transfer.

Use an evidence-producing loop by default:

1. State one concrete question and the observation that would matter.
2. Record the exact model, interface, budget, stopping condition, and evidence
   destination.
3. Build only the instrument needed to make the result interpretable.
4. Run the authorized test, inspect the retained evidence, and report what it
   supports in plain language.
5. Let the result choose the next question.

The prospective note may live in the plan, runner, or evidence directory. A
routine exploratory iteration does not need its own mechanism decision,
implementation decision, contact decision, and model-review sequence.

Exploration and validation have different jobs. Exploration is allowed to be
messy because its purpose is to discover a phenomenon worth explaining. If
repeated evidence reveals a thesis-relevant effect, validation then freezes
the strong controls: same-model baselines, prospective transfer and
non-transfer cases, external consequences, causal ablations, replication, and
claim-bearing scorers.

An import failure, unavailable provider, or other event before a participant
request is an engineering failure. It may teach the project how to repair its
apparatus, but it is not evidence about Formation and does not consume the
scientific experiment. Repair it without creating a new research program.

Cursor reviews are optional internal critiques requested by a human. They are
not external peer review and do not license routine iteration. Human or other
genuinely external criticism becomes most valuable after the lab has enough
evidence to present a real claim or a coherent body of null results.

### Why this method changed

The retained program history shows a recurring selection effect. After two
practice contacts stopped `not_engaged`, the project made model admission and
generic contract reliability the organizing problem. Successive admission,
interface, and computation gates produced valid bounded results but no contact
with development. Locally sound demands for a clean contrast had composed into
a route that required the future practitioner to be experimentally legible
before the lifecycle was allowed to develop.

The project records the following as a methodological interpretation of its
work history, not as a computed Formation finding:

> Agent-led Formation work has tended to replace developmental uncertainty
> with admission certainty, producing rigorous non-contact rather than
> evidence about development.

The completed admission ladder is now closed as negative research history. It
established useful facts about the tested setups: interface compliance and
computational competence are separate, shape constraints can repair the reply
container without repairing the answer, and several exact model interfaces
were unsuitable for their assigned gates. It did not establish that a model
must occupy an ideal developmental band before Formation may contact it.

The practical correction is:

- do not begin another model-catalog search or generic competence gate;
- choose one model operationally because it can participate in the named
  contact;
- require only the minimal interface check for the exact responsibility it
  must perform;
- keep that interface and its constraints identical across compared branches;
- treat imperfect behavior, variance, misunderstanding, and awkward learning
  opportunities as observations unless they prevent any action from being
  captured;
- run explicitly exploratory developmental contacts under small stated
  budgets;
- use that contact to locate the next experimental problem before freezing a
  validation protocol; and
- continue the deterministic lifecycle as a separate supporting lane, starting
  with environment application, without making its completion a prerequisite
  for exploration.

The last exploratory question was intentionally weaker than the Formation
thesis:

> When the same model and action interface are held fixed, what later
> behavioral differences appear after no developmental offer, raw experience,
> a runtime-authored interpretation, or a frozen explicit lesson follows one
> experience and its externally issued consequence?

That contact exposed same-request variation large enough to explain its only
branch difference. This is a useful constraint on the next experiment, but it
is not itself a discovery about development. Prospective transfer,
non-transfer, same-model baselines, external consequences, and causal
attribution remain requirements once a candidate Formation effect is ready for
validation.

The detailed review and license language later in this plan records how earlier
work was conducted. It is historical evidence about the route, not a template
or authority requirement for new exploratory work.

## Supporting lane — build the two-loop framework

Goal: make practice and formation traversable while keeping all intelligence
stubbed and all claims at wire level.

Deliverables:

- an append-only developmental lineage;
- a practice-loop port for model, tools, environment, and consequence;
- a formation-loop port that can propose, govern, suspend, and revoke a
  candidate change along the fixture-required path, with trial and richer
  lifecycle transitions remaining optional policy operations;
- replay that reconstructs runtime state from lineage;
- explicit activation receipts connecting an admitted change to a later action;
- deterministic tests for separation, lifecycle, replay, and refusal paths; and
- a trajectory harness that can fork an identical initial practitioner.

Exit condition: deterministic fixtures traverse positive activation,
non-activation, contradiction, revocation, and ablation without an LM call.
This work supports later causal audit. It does not gate the current analytical
route selection, count as model development, or establish a Formation claim.

## Next validation step — occupy one transfer problem

Goal: find a small real-engine environment where consequence can plausibly
produce a transferable change.

These requirements apply after exploration has located a concrete problem:

- the cold model makes a repeatable, consequential class of error;
- an external oracle scores outcomes without relying on model self-report;
- later cases share relevant structure but not answer tokens or superficial
  episode keys;
- there are natural cases where transfer should lose; and
- the task is cheap enough for branching, ablation, and replication.

The domain is selected for experimental leverage, not product identity. Coding,
editing, tool use, research, and game mastering are all candidates.

Exit condition: a fresh validation charter names the environment, oracle,
baselines, transfer and non-transfer families, budget, stopping rule, and
retained record. Exploratory cases and model outputs may motivate this charter
but may not be reused as prospective validation cases.

## Phase 3 — compare candidate formation mechanisms

Goal: determine whether any governed change beats simpler persistence.

Minimum branches from an identical model and initial state:

1. no persistence;
2. raw episode retrieval;
3. model-authored lesson;
4. consequence-governed candidate change; and
5. a declared public ablation of each named causal contributor.

Primary measures:

- prospective task success;
- positive and negative transfer;
- correction after counterevidence;
- activation precision and recall;
- input, latency, and checking cost; and
- causal effect of individual experiences and admitted changes.

Exit condition: a computed verdict closes the candidate as supported, null,
harmful, not engaged, or invalid within its stated evidence bounds.

## Phase 4 — developmental trajectories

Goal: move from one acquired change to cumulative practice without losing
attribution or governance.

Questions include interference between changes, skill composition, curriculum
order, decay, revalidation, recovery after bad formation, and whether later
competence reduces the need for explicit context.

This phase is not licensed by completion of the wire framework. It begins only
after at least one bounded formation mechanism survives Phase 3.

## Phase 5 — exceptional practitioners

Goal: evaluate whether accumulated formation produces material superiority in a
real practice.

The comparator remains the same cold model equipped with strong static
instructions and ordinary persistence. Evaluation uses prospective work,
external outcomes, and adversarial non-transfer cases. Domain success does not
automatically generalize to a universal architecture.
