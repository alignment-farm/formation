# Observational counterevidence comparison

Status: **frozen before contact under the session-wide human authorization**.

## Question

Can a complete exploratory action and consequence warrant a useful revision
when the selected control's movement contradicts the current record, even
though the current record did not choose that action?

This experiment compares observation-grounded and action-attributed governors.
It does not change the completed longer-lineage scorer.

## Two source-action policies

Each lineage forks from the same parent state into two source occurrences:

- **target-directed:** the participant receives the current A version-2
  sentence and chooses an action for the current target;
- **explicit exploration:** the public runtime policy commits the first valid
  displayed control to observe its effect. It receives no expected movement or
  answer and makes no participant-model call.

Both committed actions receive ordinary environment results. Any selected
control must move opposite to version 2 because the environment relation has
reversed.

The restricted clerk independently transcribes each source, writes both
effects, parses the prose, and projects the proposed effect for the observed
control. The composed source-support checks run before either governor.

## Two governors

The observation-grounded governor admits a composed-supported proposal when
the complete external movement contradicts version 2's claim for the selected
control.

The action-attributed governor requires the same facts and also requires a
target-directed source whose committed action equals the action version 2
recommended for that target. It always quarantines the explicitly exploratory
source.

Action origin is public runtime configuration. Neither governor receives a
later score, expected action, hidden branch label, or counterfactual result.

## Frozen verdict

The apparatus is engaged only if supplied guidance makes at least 43 of 48
matching actions, at least 21 of 24 in each design and direction, and every
supplied and observation-grounded-exploration participant cell contains at
least two valid actions. Invalid outputs elsewhere remain wrong.

Observation-grounded exploratory revision is supported only if:

- the exact parent packet and all eight parent version-2 admissions are bound;
- all four exploratory sources commit the first valid control and yield a
  complete movement contradicting version 2's selected-effect claim;
- at least three of four exploratory version-3 records and projections are
  exact and composed-admitted;
- the observation-grounded governor admits all four exploratory proposals and
  the action-attributed governor quarantines all four;
- target-directed and exploratory clerks produce equal exact record content in
  at least three of four lineages;
- at least 20 of 24 later normalizations and selections are exact, with no more
  than two false unrelated selections;
- observation-grounded exploratory revisions make at least 43 of 48 matching
  actions and at least 21 of 24 in each design and direction;
- they guide at least 21 of 24 A actions while unchanged version 2 guides at
  least 21 of 24 B actions;
- they exceed cold, raw exploration, action-attributed exploration, and removal
  by at least 16 matching actions each;
- they trail supplied guidance by no more than four actions;
- every later version assignment matches the prospective A-version-3,
  B-version-2, or unrelated-empty role; and
- they lose no more than three unrelated actions relative to cold.

Target-directed admission and action attribution are retained diagnostics, not
requirements for the observational verdict. If the apparatus is engaged but
the observational exploratory branch loses at least six unrelated actions, the
verdict is `harmful`. Otherwise it is `supported` or `null`.

This test can support learning a revision from deliberate exploration. It
cannot attribute the exploratory action to version 2, establish superiority
over supplied guidance, prove large-catalog or long-horizon behavior, or
establish Formation.
