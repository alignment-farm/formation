# Learned clerical instrument validation

Status: **frozen before contact under the session-wide human authorization**.

## What is being validated

The exploratory chain found a bounded clerical mechanism:

1. A restricted 4B model transcribes visible features and an observed
   consequence.
2. Another cold call states both control effects in prose.
3. A parser call extracts those two explicit facts into named fields.
4. A deterministic serializer puts the model-written fields into a participant-
   usable sentence.
5. The clerk normalizes a later device's visible features.
6. The runtime compares both model-written feature fields for exact equality
   and delivers the sentence only on a complete match.
7. A separate cold 14B participant chooses the action.

The earlier candidate assembled retained outputs from more than one contact.
This validation runs the whole chain fresh and freezes its causal comparisons
before contact.

## Question

Does fresh consequence-exposed clerical processing produce useful, selective
guidance on later devices with new identities and controls, relative to cold,
raw, consequence-hidden, removal, unselected, and supplied controls?

This validates learned instrumentation around a cold participant. It does not
test whether one model develops alone.

## Fresh worlds

Four independent lineages each contain two source designs with opposite
control relations. The same visible feature vocabulary appears with balanced
relations across lineages, so no one visible feature always predicts one
effect across the validation.

Each lineage later receives six devices: A above, A below, B above, B below, a
wholly novel design, and a recombination containing one feature from each
source. Later family IDs, device IDs, positions, and control strings are all
fresh. Matching depends on the pair of visible features, not identity.

The environment owns and applies the true control effects. It produces the
source consequence and scores later action.

## Consequence-hidden control

Every source action produces one environment result. The exposed chain receives
the selected actuator and whether the gauge rose or fell. The hidden chain
receives the same device description and selected actuator, but the gauge
movement is replaced by a fixed unavailable statement.

Both chains receive the same number and kinds of clerical calls. Their exact
outputs are retained. A malformed intermediate remains malformed and later
calls still occur on schedule.

## Later comparisons

Each later case receives three identical participant calls under nine
conditions:

- cold, with no retained material;
- both exposed raw sensory reports;
- the complete exposed clerical pipeline;
- the complete consequence-hidden clerical pipeline;
- the exposed pipeline with selected guidance removed before action;
- both exposed rendered sentences without applicability selection;
- a supplied correct sentence selected by clerk normalization and exact scope
  equality;
- the exposed model sentence selected by the environment-owned structural
  match; and
- a supplied sentence selected by the environment-owned structural match.

The last branch is the participant ceiling. The supplied normalized branch
isolates later normalization. The oracle model branch isolates experience
encoding and participant consumption. The hidden and removal branches support
causal attribution. The novel and recombined cases test non-transfer.

## Frozen verdict

The apparatus is not engaged unless the oracle-selected supplied sentence
makes at least 43 of 48 matching actions, at least 21 of 24 in each design and
direction, and every three-call participant cell contains at least two valid
action objects.

The validation is supported only if all of these hold:

- at least seven of eight exposed transcriptions are exact;
- at least seven of eight exposed prose accounts state both effects correctly;
- at least seven of eight parsed effect records are exact and render exactly;
- at least 20 of 24 later scope normalizations are exact;
- exact matching over model scopes selects correctly in at least 20 of 24
  cases, with no more than two false selections across eight novel or
  recombined cases;
- the full exposed pipeline makes at least 43 of 48 matching actions;
- it makes at least 21 of 24 in each design and direction;
- it exceeds cold, raw, consequence-hidden, and removal by at least 16 matching
  actions each;
- it trails the oracle-selected supplied ceiling by no more than four actions;
- oracle-selected model guidance and normalized supplied guidance each make at
  least 43 matching actions; and
- it loses no more than three unrelated actions relative to cold.

If the apparatus is engaged but the full pipeline loses at least six unrelated
actions, the verdict is `harmful`. Otherwise the frozen verdict is `supported`
or `null`.

The exact selection and rendering mechanisms are part of the declared
formation substrate. A supported result would validate this bounded learned
clerical instrument. Formation would remain null because the experiment does
not test revision, longer accumulation, net value, or practice beyond this
small control domain.

## Budget and stopping rule

The schedule contains 728 logical calls:

- eight participant source actions;
- 24 exposed clerical calls across transcription, prose, and parsing;
- 24 matched consequence-hidden clerical calls;
- 24 later normalization calls; and
- 648 later participant calls.

At most 12 transport failures may be retried. The physical ceiling is 740
attempts. Model output never changes the schedule. A valid output is never
resampled. The runner stops after the fixed schedule or either ceiling.

Evidence is written under
`evidence/learned-clerical-instrument-validation-<run-id>/` and replayed from
retained raw requests and responses before successful exit.
