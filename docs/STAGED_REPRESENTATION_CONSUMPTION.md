# Staged sentence and table consumption

Status: **frozen before contact under the session-wide human authorization**.

## Question

When the same exact model-written observation is converted into a relation
sentence and an effect table, does one form guide later cold-model action more
reliably?

The balanced staged validation wrote every observation and table correctly but
missed its verdict because one upward action cell failed. The supplied correct
table also struggled in that cell. This experiment therefore tests the lesson
interface. It does not retest whether the consequence caused the observation.

## Comparison

Six fresh source families are fixed before contact. Three have a first action
that increases position. Three have a second action that increases position.
The model acts once in each source and receives the environment-issued selected
slot and movement. A second cold call writes one exact observation of that
result.

Two separate cold authors receive that same observation. One writes a single
relation sentence. The other writes a JSON effect table. Both must infer the
unobserved slot from the fact that one action increases position and the other
decreases it. The harness preserves their text exactly and does not repair it.

Each source then receives four new cases: targets above and below in the same
controller family, and targets above and below in a new family with the opposite
relation. Every branch and case receives three identical calls.

The eight later branches are no retained information, raw experience, the
model-written sentence with a family check, the model-written table with the
same check, each model-written form without the check, and supplied exact
sentence and table controls with the check.

The harness may apply the action, preserve exact model text, compare exact
family identity, and score later actions. It may not infer a lesson, choose an
action from a lesson, or select a favorable model sample.

## Frozen interpretation

The experiment is `not_engaged` unless all six observations, sentences, and
tables are exact and each supplied representation makes at least 30 of 36
matching actions.

A representation is usable only if:

- its model-written form makes at least 30 of 36 matching actions;
- it makes at least 14 of 18 matching actions in each movement direction and
  each true-relation group;
- it trails its supplied exact control by no more than three matching actions;
- with the family check, it loses no more than two unrelated actions compared
  with no retained information;
- the family check prevents at least ten errors caused by unchecked delivery;
  and
- every three-call branch and case contains at least two valid action objects.

If neither form is usable, the result is null. If only one is usable, that form
is preferred. If both are usable, one is preferred only when it makes at least
four more matching actions than the other without losing more unrelated
actions. Otherwise both remain usable.

This is an interface result. It cannot establish a Formation effect because it
does not include consequence withholding, removal of the authored lesson, or
revision.
