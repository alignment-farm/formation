## Frozen verdict

The apparatus is engaged only if supplied guidance makes at least 43 of 48
matching actions, at least 21 of 24 in each design and target direction, and
every supplied and composed-current three-call cell contains at least two valid
actions. All invalid output remains wrong in its fixed denominator.

The selective longer-lineage mechanism is supported only if:

- the retained parent packet and all eight parent version-2 admissions match
  their frozen hashes and fields;
- the two current version-2 records make at least 43 of 48 pre-change actions;
- all four third-occurrence actions follow version 2 into a contradictory
  result;
- at least three of four exposed version-3 records and projections are exact;
- all four exposed version-3 proposals are admitted;
- all four stale version-2 and all four movement-hidden proposals are
  quarantined;
- at least 20 of 24 new normalizations and selections are exact, with no more
  than two false novel or recombined selections;
- the composed current catalog makes at least 43 of 48 matching actions and at
  least 21 of 24 in each design and target direction;
- version 3 guides at least 21 of 24 A actions while the unchanged version 2
  guides at least 21 of 24 B actions;
- the current catalog exceeds cold, raw, stale, hidden, and removal by at least
  16 matching actions each;
- it trails supplied guidance by no more than four actions;
- environment-selected clerk records make at least 43 matching actions;
- every composed-current matching action is assigned A version 3 or B version
  2 as prospectively specified, and no record is assigned to an unrelated
  case; and
- the composed current catalog loses no more than three unrelated actions
  relative to cold.

If the apparatus is engaged but the current catalog loses at least six
unrelated actions, the verdict is `harmful`. Otherwise it is `supported` or
`null`.

This experiment can support selective chronology across a three-version record
and a coexisting untouched record. It cannot establish long-horizon durability,
large-catalog behavior, superiority over supplied guidance, net value, or
Formation.
